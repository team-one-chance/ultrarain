# Modding ULTRARAIN

Community mods are welcome! This includes gameplay changes, new weapons, enemies, mechanics and levels, visual and audio changes, total conversions that completely transform the game, and noncommercial community forks and experiments.

## Share your work

You may distribute independently created noncommercial mods and patch files. Mods can require a legitimate installation of ULTRARAIN and can modify original assets locally under the permission in [ASSET-LICENSE.md](ASSET-LICENSE.md).

Keep protected original game assets out of your mod package. Share your own replacement art, audio and levels, or patches that apply to the player's installation without carrying redistributed copies of protected originals. A patch file is not an exception to the asset restrictions if it contains those assets. Obtain the relevant owner's permission for any third-party content you include.

## Code forks and game assets have different rules

ULTRARAIN's original source code is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). It permits noncommercial modifications and redistribution, including code forks, subject to its terms. When sharing covered code, include the license text or its URL and the required notice:

`Required Notice: Copyright 2026 Team One Chance`

The code license also defines other permitted purposes; its exact terms govern. It does not generally license ULTRARAIN's protected artwork, audio, or creative level content. This distinction also applies inside mixed GameMaker `.yy` files: programming metadata and creative content can have different coverage. See the scope table in [ASSET-LICENSE.md](ASSET-LICENSE.md). A whole-project fork or compiled game may include protected assets even when its code can be shared.

## Ownership and presentation

You retain ownership of your original contributions. Respect the licenses of code or other material your work incorporates.

Present unofficial mods clearly as unofficial; do not imply endorsement by Team One Chance. This is a condition of the local asset-modding permission, not an additional restriction on the PolyForm code license.

Commercial use of Team One Chance material outside the permissions already granted requires separate permission from Team One Chance. Third-party material requires permission under its own terms.

These guidelines explain the separate code and asset permissions. They do not override or narrow PolyForm, limit rights under applicable law, or automatically govern independently authored software containing no protected ULTRARAIN material.
