We started off by brainstorming ideas. Stuff that involves programming. We eventually converged on a videogame. After about half an hour of discussing whether to make a platformer or a deckbuilder, we decided on a fast paced 2d platformer with synergies between items and weapons. You can get items that change or improve your character or their weapons. A mix of ULTRAKILL and Risk of Rain, which is where I got the name ULTARARAIN

i started off by installing git on everyone's computers. i made a github organization called Team One Chance (team-one-chance) and made a repository. Maksym added an empty GameMaker project to the repo. I made a Github Project and, with Maksym, made the entire MVP document where we detail what the baseline of the game should be. I used ChatGPT for the questions and answered them with Maksym.
